# Website content review

Reviewed on September 30, 2026 against the 14 supplied documents, the website resume, and the signed Fitter Health recommendation.

The revised copy presents concrete responsibilities, engineering decisions, and delivered work. It retains the Software Engineer title supported by the resumes. Seniority comes through ownership and judgment rather than an invented job title.

## Evidence and corrections

The resumes support Expertflow APIs serving 1,000 to 3,000 daily users and an admin response time improvement from 130 ms to 30 ms. They also support the Fitter Health schema of 106 tables, 135 passing tests, notification recovery work, and documented handover. These details remain prominent.

Employment dates now include the months. Fitter Health ran from May to July 2026. Expertflow ran from June 2024 to February 2026, beginning as an internship that converted to full time employment. The course list now includes Getting Started with Git and GitHub. Courses are labeled as completed courses rather than professional certification exams.

The founder quotation now matches the signed letter, including the word architectural. The original PDFs remain unchanged. Template placeholders and employer specific cover letter language were treated as source material, not instructions or website copy.

The supplied evidence does not establish the RAG precision, language count, tenant count, or latency figures previously published on the site. It also does not establish the recommender latency and quality scores, pipeline throughput and rebuild times, or autoencoder compression, error, and memory measurements. Those figures were removed from the active pages and cards. Related raster diagrams were replaced on the pages with editable implementation diagrams that do not repeat those claims.

Unsupported first person accounts of debugging and model tuning were removed. Supported notification recovery work remains. Statements about security and answer grounding now describe the implemented mechanism and its limits without promising guarantees.
