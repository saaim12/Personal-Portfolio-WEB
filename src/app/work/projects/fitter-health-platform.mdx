## The brief

Fitter Health needed a preventive health platform for a team based in Spain. As the sole engineer on the contract, I delivered the application, backend services, cloud infrastructure, and data workflows. The engagement ran from May to July 2026 and ended with a documented handover.

The platform connected patient and provider accounts, membership credits, session booking, commerce, and an admin CRM. Odoo handled invoicing, with payment status synchronized back to the platform.

## What I built

I built Django and Django REST Framework services over a PostgreSQL schema of 106 tables and connected them to a Next.js frontend. The data model covered membership entitlements, credit balances, booking, orders, and operational records.

Celery and Redis handled background work. I deployed containerized services on AWS ECS with RDS and S3, using IAM and VPC controls to scope access. EventBridge, Lambda, and SES supported scheduled renewal reminders and member engagement email.

I also delivered an ETL pipeline into a PostgreSQL warehouse with a star schema. It provided a shared data model for ML features, product analytics, and aggregated partner reporting.

## Fixing notification delivery

The member engagement workflow was losing notifications without a clear recovery path. I traced the issue to the delivery path between SNS and its consumers and added SQS buffering.

The revised flow gave consumers queued work to retry. Repeated failures went to a dead letter queue, and queue depth alarms made stalled processing visible. This addressed the implementation's missing recovery path and gave the team a way to inspect failed work.

## Engineering decisions

**Keep business rules close to the data.** Membership credits, booking, and invoice status involve related writes. Database integrity constraints and migrations helped protect those relationships across application workflows.

**Separate request handling from background work.** Celery and Redis supported application tasks while scheduled AWS workflows handled member lifecycle email. Each path needed clear failure handling and operational ownership.

**Make the system transferable.** I documented the API surface and delivered 135 passing tests. The handover needed to give the team enough context to operate and extend the platform after the contract ended.

## Result

I delivered the platform and its supporting infrastructure, resolved the notification recovery issue, and handed over the documented system. The founder's recommendation describes my backend, cloud, and data architecture work, along with how I communicated decisions and prepared the handover.

For a similar engagement, I would review retry behavior, failure visibility, and recovery procedures during the initial design. Those decisions affect both implementation and the team's ability to operate the product.
